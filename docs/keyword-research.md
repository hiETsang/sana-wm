## What SANA-WM Is
SANA-WM is an open-source world model from NVIDIA Research that turns one image plus a camera trajectory into minute-scale, camera-controlled video.

Key boundaries:
- It is a world model, not a generic text-to-video landing page.
- It focuses on long-horizon spatial consistency and camera control.
- It generates minute-scale 720p video rather than a short cinematic clip only.
- It is research software first; this site is an independent guide, not the official project page.

## Verified Advantages
- Faster: distilled inference can denoise a 60s 720p clip in about 34s on one RTX 5090.
- More controllable: the model follows precise 6-DoF camera trajectories.
- More coherent: hybrid linear attention and a second-stage refiner target long-video consistency.
- More practical to evaluate: official demos, paper, and repo are already public.
